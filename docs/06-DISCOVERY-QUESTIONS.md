# 06 — Questions to settle with Mum

Take this to the shop. The first six answers determine the database and main screens; the rest refine launch scope.

1. Confirmed: **cases are pillow cases**; **shia may come with curtains or sell separately**. Please show one real shia sample so its fabric and finished form are named accurately. What is **“square lumper”**?
2. Confirmed: customers may buy **a set or one curtain piece**, with or without shia. How many curtain pieces normally make one set, and can that number vary by window?
3. Confirmed: customers can request **fabric by the metre**. Which material types are sold this way, and can a remnant be sold separately?
4. What details uniquely distinguish a fabric: design/code, colour, pattern, width, supplier, price tier? Can two rolls with the same design and colour have different costs?
   - Add examples of the shop's **material types** and **texture/finish** names so the catalogue uses the words staff already know.
5. At what point is stock committed to a customer: quotation, deposit, confirmed order, or cutting? How long may a reservation remain?
6. How does she currently calculate required fabric for a curtain: width, drop, fullness, pleats, seam allowance and shrinkage? Who approves the actual cut?
7. What are the usual payment terms: deposit percentage, balance at pickup, credit customers, partial payments, refunds?
8. Does Trendy Collections already have its **own Paybill** (or Till), business M-Pesa admin access and a Daraja developer account? A personal M-Pesa number is not the same integration path.
9. Does she have one shop/store today? Is there a separate cutting room, tailor or warehouse that needs stock transfers?
10. How many people will use the system, on what devices, and how reliable is internet at the shop?
    - List each person's role and whether cutting, sewing and checking are done by different people. When you say someone sewed “20 metres”, do you mean metres of fabric consumed or the finished curtain width? The dashboard can show both separately once the shop confirms its working definition.
11. Are ready-made curtains/cases purchased from suppliers as finished stock, sewn in-house, or both?
12. What reports does she actually use weekly: best-selling colours, rolls nearing end, unpaid orders, tailor output, profit, tax invoices?

## Suggested 45-minute discovery session

- **10 min:** walk through an actual incoming roll and its label.
- **10 min:** walk through a recent customer measurement and sewing order.
- **10 min:** walk through a ready-stock sale and a fabric-by-metre sale.
- **10 min:** compare one M-Pesa receipt with her sale record and end-of-day close.
- **5 min:** agree the first screens and who signs off stock balances.

Bring 2–3 real examples of each product type, a recent supplier invoice, a customer receipt/order book page (with private details hidden for demos), and current roll measurements. This will let the first implementation use real units and names.
